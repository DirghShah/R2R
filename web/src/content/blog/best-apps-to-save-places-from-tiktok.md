Every one of these apps promises the same thing: stop losing restaurants in your
saved folder. They differ in how they get the place out of the video, and that
difference decides whether they work on the reels you actually save.

We make one of them, so treat the last entry accordingly. The rest is written to
be useful rather than flattering.

## The thing that actually separates them

**How does the app find the place?** There are three answers, and they predict
almost everything else.

1. **You type it.** The app is a nice list with a map. Fast and reliable, but it
   is you doing the work.
2. **It reads a screenshot.** OCR on one frame. No platform access needed, but
   it sees one moment and hears nothing.
3. **It reads the whole video.** Caption, on-screen text across many frames,
   audio, tagged accounts. Slower and more expensive to run, and the only
   approach that handles a reel listing nine places.

## Beli

**What it is:** a restaurant ranking and social app. You log where you have
eaten, rank it against everywhere else you have been, and see friends' rankings.

**Strength:** by far the best social graph in this category, and the ranking
mechanic is genuinely fun — comparing two restaurants head to head produces a
better list than star ratings ever do.

**Not what it is for:** Beli is about places you have *been*, not places you saw
in a video. There is no reel import. If your problem is a backlog of saved
TikToks, this does not solve it.

## Stasht

**What it is:** a general "saves that work" app — places, events, products,
recipes — across mobile, desktop and a browser extension.

**Strength:** it reads captions, on-screen text and speech, so it handles
multi-place reels properly. The scope is broader than food; events land on your
calendar.

**Tradeoff:** that breadth means it is not built specifically around a map. If
all you want is restaurants on a map, some of it is surface area you will not
use.

## Corner

**What it is:** curated place discovery, with a strong editorial and
city-guide feel.

**Strength:** the taste level is high, and the curated lists are genuinely worth
browsing on their own.

**Tradeoff:** more oriented toward discovering what others have curated than
processing your own backlog.

## Plotline

**What it is:** travel-focused saving from TikTok and Instagram, organised by
trip.

**Strength:** the trip framing is right for travel specifically — grouping by
destination beats one enormous map when you are planning a week in Lisbon.

**Tradeoff:** less suited to the everyday "where should we eat tonight" case.

## Rodeo

**What it is:** from an ex-Hinge team. Extraction from screenshots and web pages,
plus a map, an agenda view and shared lists.

**Strength:** the screenshot approach sidesteps platform APIs entirely, and the
shared-list-for-the-group-chat framing is the right social model. It also
searches reservations.

**Tradeoff:** a screenshot is one frame with no audio. Reels that flash a name
for two seconds, or where the recommendation is spoken, are exactly the ones it
will miss.

## Nosh

**What it is:** ours. Share a reel from Instagram, TikTok or YouTube; it reads
the caption, samples frames for on-screen text, transcribes the audio, and pins
every place it finds with the address, hours, tips and what to order.

**What we built it around:** completeness. A reel captioned "9 cafés in Dallas"
should produce nine pins, not one. Everything else follows from that — reading
frames and audio costs more than reading a caption, but a caption alone loses
most of the list.

**Also:** any map can be shared by link, so the group chat builds it together.
Non-food reels — meal-kit ads, recipes, product promos — are rejected rather
than pinned.

**Honest limitations:** analysis takes around thirty seconds. Chain restaurants
sometimes match the wrong branch, and you fix that by dragging the pin. It is
iPhone only.

## How to choose

- **You want to rank places you have been:** Beli.
- **You save more than restaurants:** Stasht.
- **You are planning a specific trip:** Plotline.
- **You want curated discovery:** Corner.
- **You prefer screenshots over sharing links:** Rodeo.
- **Your backlog is multi-place food reels:** something that reads the whole
  video, ours included.

The honest advice: try two. They are all free to start, and fifteen minutes with
your own saved folder will tell you more than any comparison table — including
this one.
